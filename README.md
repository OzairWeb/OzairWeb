## Choosing the right starting point

**A business website:** start with the questions a customer needs answered and the action they should take. WordPress suits teams that need familiar editing tools; a custom application is useful when permissions, records or integrations become part of the product.

**An online store:** choose the platform around your catalogue and operating process. Shopify fits many managed storefronts. WooCommerce connects commerce to an existing WordPress site. A custom storefront needs a clear reason, such as unusual product logic or a workflow that standard tools cannot support.

**A SaaS product or internal tool:** define one complete user journey before expanding the feature list. Authentication, authorization, validation, recovery states and deployment instructions belong in that first usable release.

**An AI or automation project:** identify the task, approved data and decisions that require human review. A useful system needs a way to handle failed integrations and uncertain answers, alongside the successful path.

**An existing site that is struggling:** diagnose the cause before rebuilding. Editing problems, slow pages, weak content structure and integration failures need different fixes. Search visibility is assessed through observable changes; rankings and AI citations cannot be guaranteed.

Our practical verdict: choose the smallest maintainable solution that completes the required journey, and make ownership and support clear before development starts.

## How a project moves forward

1. **Understand the job:** discuss users, content, current systems and the outcome the project needs to improve.
2. **Agree the scope:** record deliverables, dependencies, acceptance criteria and the support arrangement.
3. **Design the journey:** work through screens, navigation and useful content before the implementation grows.
4. **Build in stages:** review working sections and resolve integration or data questions early.
5. **Check the release:** test the important journeys, responsive behavior and agreed requirements.
6. **Launch and hand over:** document operation, ownership and the route for ongoing improvements.
